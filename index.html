What does Headnote Studio do?

Headnote Studio is a marketing studio for small and mid-size UK law firms, based in Solihull. We measure how a firm appears when people ask ChatGPT, Google's AI and Google's local results for a solicitor, then run the visibility, review and content work needed to appear more often - with every claim in our reports tagged as evidence, inference or assumption.

Which AI tools and search surfaces do you measure?

The core surfaces are ChatGPT, Google's AI (AI Overviews and AI Mode) and Google's local results. We also check Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity weekly. Every search is run the same way each time - logged out, dated and repeated across runs - because a single search proves nothing.

Do you guarantee rankings or new clients?

No - and we would suggest being wary of anyone who does. No one controls Google's AI or ChatGPT. Where we offer guarantees, they are refunds of our own fee against work we control, judged from a dated baseline. Measurable work, honestly reported.

Is what you publish SRA-compliant?

We are a marketing studio, not a law firm, so we are not SRA-authorised - but we work within the SRA Code of Conduct and build everything around it. What we publish for you is information for the public, never legal advice, and it is written to be accurate and not misleading. Your nominated partner signs off every piece before it publishes, so your firm keeps full control of its own regulatory position.

Who does Headnote Studio work with?

Small and mid-size UK law firms serving individuals and businesses in their local area - typically in conveyancing, wills and probate, family law, employment law and dispute resolution. We work exclusively with the legal sector.
