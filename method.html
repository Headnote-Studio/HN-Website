Our method

Evidence, not promises

Nobody controls Google's AI or ChatGPT, and nobody honest guarantees rankings. Here is what we measure, how we measure it, and what we promise instead.

§

Every claim is tagged

Everything in our reports is marked evidence (verified, cited, screenshotted with dates), inference (a reasoned conclusion from that evidence) or assumption (awaiting your confirmation). You always see what we know versus what we think.

%

One honest number

We agree the searches that matter to your firm and run each of them monthly through the four AI tools and Google — the same way each time, logged out, dated. You see the share of those searches in which your firm is named, moving (or not) over time. You can check it yourself; we'd encourage it.

£

We guarantee our fee, never the engines

Where we offer guarantees, they are refunds of our own fee against things we control. Improved, measured, monitored presence, honestly reported either way — that's the promise.
