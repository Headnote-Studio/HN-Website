Every claim is tagged

Everything in our reports is marked evidence (verified, cited, screenshotted with dates), inference (a reasoned conclusion from that evidence) or assumption (awaiting your confirmation). You always see what we know versus what we think.

One honest number

We agree the searches that matter to your firm and run each of them monthly through the AI engines and Google - the same way each time, logged out, dated. You see the share of those searches in which your firm is named, moving (or not) over time. You can check it yourself; we'd encourage it.

£

We guarantee our fee, never the engines

Where we offer guarantees, they are refunds of our own fee against things we control, judged from a dated baseline. Improved, measured, monitored presence, honestly reported either way - that's the promise.

What we test

The surfaces that matter, tested the same way every time.

The core surfaces are ChatGPT, Google's AI (AI Overviews and AI Mode) and Google's local results - with Claude, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity checked weekly alongside.

Every search is run logged out, dated and repeated across runs, because AI answers are volatile and a single search proves nothing. A baseline is at least three captures over two or more weeks; anything less is noise, and we say so.

As the engines shift - and they do - we test for it and publish what we find, rather than guessing.

Everything we measure about your firm, we measure about ourselves.

Plain answers

Questions,
answered.

Why do you run each search several times?

Because AI answers are volatile - the same question can produce a different shortlist run to run. A single search proves nothing, so a baseline is at least three captures over two or more weeks, logged out and dated. Anything less is noise, and we say so.

What is the one honest number?

The share of your agreed priority searches in which your firm is named, measured monthly across the same engines, the same way each time. You see it moving - or not - over time, and you can check it yourself; we'd encourage it.

Why won't you guarantee rankings?

Because no one controls Google's AI or ChatGPT, and engine behaviour changes without notice. A ranking guarantee is a liability dressed up as a promise. What we guarantee is our own fee: measurable work, honestly reported, refunded where we say it will be.

How will we know it's working?

We review the leading indicators together against the dated baseline - including at day 90 of any monthly engagement. If the honest answer is “not yet”, that is what the report says.
