Who qualifies for the free AI-Search Snapshot?

UK law firms. Request it from your firm's email address with your firm name, town and main practice areas. We run a limited number of snapshots each month - if this month's are taken, you go to the front of next month's list.

Why must the request come from our firm's email address?

The snapshot is free and we run a limited number each month, so we keep them for real firms. It also protects our no-follow-up promise: your request is tagged so you are never added to a sales sequence.

What happens after we receive the snapshot?

Nothing, unless you ask. There is no follow-up sales sequence. If you want the full picture, the next step is the Visibility Report - benchmarked against three named local competitors, with a ten-minute video walkthrough.
